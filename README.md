## Projects Showcase

### 1. California Housing Price Analysis & Prediction
- **Research Question**: What are the key geographic and economic indicators driving median house values, and how effectively can a regression model predict housing prices?
- **Status**: Completed
- **Key Visualizations & Findings**:
  - *Finding 1*: Median income showed the strongest positive correlation with median house values, acting as the most dominant predictor.
  - *Finding 2*: Geographic coordinates and population proximity significantly impact pricing, reflecting strong coastal premiums.
